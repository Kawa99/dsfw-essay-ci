About this service

This tool helps teams across the Welsh public sector understand their existing digital skills and identify where support or training is needed.

What you will need

  • A public sector work email address (for example, NHS, local authority or Welsh Government)
  • The name or code of your team so you can join your manager’s team
  • Around 10 minutes to answer simple yes/no questions about your work

How it works

  1. Sign in using your work email.
  2. Join your manager’s team using the code or link they provide.
  3. Complete the digital skills self assessment.
  4. View your results and recommended resources to help fill any skill gaps.
