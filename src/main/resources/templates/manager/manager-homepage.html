Guidance for Managers

Understanding your team's skills

The Team Overview page provides a heatmap of your team's current digital capabilities. Use this data to identify:

  • Common skill gaps across the entire team
  • Individuals who may need specific training
  • Champions who can mentor others

Managing your roster

To remove a staff member who has left the team, navigate to the Team Overview page and select the "Remove" option next to their name.

Data Privacy

Remember that self-assessment data is sensitive. Only use these results to support development conversations, not for performance management.
